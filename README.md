# Traversal Prototype 
Traversal 


This is a Geuddy Perez First-Person, Platformer, Movement Prototype.


WASD is for Movement.

Mouse can control that camera. Fixed Sensitive is at 200

Spacebar = Jump | Holding Space near walls fixes players towards the wall with their current velocity. It stores then with a multiplier, increase the speed of the original movement speed when on Wall. The more momentum and velocity player has, the more movement speed will be transferred from Wall to Wall.

Pressing Space on a wall, always players to kick off the wall. 

What went well?

Getting the movement and the first-person camera control was fairly easy. Also applying jump wasn't too bad. 


What didn’t go well?

Figuring out how to get the wall running. It took a while from understand each state that the player needs to be. I've done Megaman X-like wall jumping before which isn't the issue, but my main goal
was to create a Wall Run feel, similar to the Titanfall and COD BO3 games. 

What challenges did you face? (Technical, creative, or production)

Definitely, figuring out the math for the wall running to properly stick and calculate player velocity. Then afterwards, the challenge was increasing the speed of the wallrun without messing with the player settings because Wall-Run should be a tool that the player can use to increase velocity. That's why I had to add a multiplier after googling all the math for the Physics to enable the Wall Run.

Also, undertanding Camera Yaw and how to apply that camera tilt was very difficult, and making sure the camera does not bug out and sticks to a fixed angles that the player can only use was very difficult and time consuming.

What can you do differently next time?


As with what I struggle with most projects and assignments, it's about Time Allocation. I don't think what I was going for was fairly difficult or should take to long, but not properly,
setting enough time has always been a challenge, more so in this version of myself, then previous versions because before I had more free time, but navigating 5 Classes and Two Jobs, plus relationships and fun 
family dynamics, I can't really use them as an excuse to not produce better work.

I often wonder if being in a better situation would attribute to getting more motivation, but I think the answer lies in just doing it and setting even an hour to get more productive work out each day, instead of 5-6 of bad productive work in one day. 

We're still a work in progress it seems.
